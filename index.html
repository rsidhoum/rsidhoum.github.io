About me

I am a PhD student in automated reasoning under the supervision of David Delahaye and Simon Robillard at the University of Montpellier, France.
I'm interested in automated reasoning and am developing Pgeon, a tool for automatically generating theorem provers from a description of their system (syntax, rules, strategy).
